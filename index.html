The training roadmap

Progress with
a purpose.

Training can develop from controlled movement to strength and power. Your plan is adapted to your goals and readiness, then reviewed as you progress.

01 / FOUNDATION

Stabilization
Endurance

Build control, balance, and muscular endurance with deliberate movement.

02 / CAPACITY

Strength
Endurance

Pair strength work with stability challenges to build repeatable capacity.

03 / GROWTH

Muscular
Development

Use planned training volume to support muscle development and strength.

04 / FORCE

Maximal
Strength

Build the ability to produce force through focused, progressive lifting.

05 / SPEED

Power

Develop fast force production when it fits your goals and training base.

Inspired by NASM’s OPT™ five-phase framework. Training phase and exercise selection are individualized; progression is based on assessment, goals, and readiness.

Your first steps

From curious to
coached.

STEP 01

Tell me about you

Complete the Habitual intake form with your goals, experience, preferences, and best way to reach you.

STEP 02

Connect

We’ll follow up to discuss your goals, coaching option, schedule, and any questions before getting started.

STEP 03

Screen and assess

Complete the current official PAR‑Q+ screening process and discuss relevant results. If follow-up is indicated, we’ll follow its guidance before training.

STEP 04

Build your plan

Agree on goals and a starting plan. We’ll track sessions, training progress, and check-ins, then adjust together.
